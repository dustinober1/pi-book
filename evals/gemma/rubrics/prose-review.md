# Blinded Gemma prose review rubric

Review only the opaque samples in `gemma-prose-review-kit.md`. Do not open the label
seal until every score is recorded.

For each sample, score each dimension from 1 (unacceptable) to 5 (strong):

- Fidelity: the prose uses the supplied authoritative facts without inventing outcomes.
- Dramatization: action, perception, and choice carry the beat instead of summary alone.
- Coherence: the passage is locally clear and internally consistent.
- Style control: the prose is specific and readable without conspicuous repetition.

Mark a severe failure when the passage contradicts its governing facts, imports a
forbidden outcome, exposes evaluation instructions, or is unusable as scene prose.
Record the opaque sample ID, scores, severe-failure decision, and a concise note.
